About Us

Born in a Lagos kitchen. Now brewing across the city.

It started with one stubborn roasting pan and a stubborn belief: Lagos deserves coffee that tastes like the city feels — full of life. So we roast in small batches, buy beans directly from growers, and never let a bag sit around longer than a week.

Every cup is ground to order, brewed with filtered water, and handed over (or dropped off) warm. No shortcuts, no syrups hiding stale beans — just coffee we'd proudly serve our own family.
